## Context

Measured: `#93A682` is 2.62:1 on white (too low for text or UI), 6.45:1 under dark ink `#1d1d1b`, 7.16:1 on the dark background `#111214`.

## Decision

`#93A682` is the accent token: a fill with dark ink text in light and dark mode, and accent text on dark backgrounds; text on light backgrounds uses a darker shade of it, the exact shade open ([OQ-16](../risks-and-open.md#oq-16-the-house-colours-text-shade)).
