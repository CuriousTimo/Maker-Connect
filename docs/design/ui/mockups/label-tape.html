Tape-1

Free

Tape printer (LabelManager PnP kind) · agent (USB) · nothing shared with the label printer

As a member (Sam): you may print a tape on Tape-1.

As an admin or owner: the same, and you change its settings.

PrintSettings
Drawer 14 · M3 bolts

Pictures are made with the label editor in Prepare (not in these mockups); this page only prints them.

mc.label.print {picture, tape_size_mm} · job.start; sizes are the ones the port declares (samples); editor: Prepare, #6, OQ-14

Offline not connected

Printing while a tape prints: Print waits until it is done.
